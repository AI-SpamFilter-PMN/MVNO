End-to-end wideband call through the MVNO rig (Kamailio 5066 + rtpengine relay),
proving G.722/16000 is genuinely negotiated and relayed — not a unit-only proof.

run `sip_traffic_sim.py --codec g722` inside `mvno_mvno_net` containers.

a=rtpmap:9 G722/16000           a=rtpmap:9 G722/16000

(G.722, 16 kHz) is the negotiated codec, PCMU/8000 kept as fallback.

## How
`sip_traffic_sim.py --codec g722` (commit `c941800`) adds the G.722 offer/answer
path: `m=audio N RTP/AVP 9 0` + `a=rtpmap:9 G722/16000` + `a=rtpmap:0 PCMU/8000`.
rtpengine relays codec-agnostic (no transcoding), so G.722 passes through and is
recorded.

This is the previously-missing "live over-the-wire G.722" proof (Phase-1 2d
strong option).
